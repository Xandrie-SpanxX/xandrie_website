## 3. COMPENSATION

The Contractor shall receive **seventy percent (70%)** of all gross revenue generated through the Contractor's Services on the Omerta Angels platform. The Company retains thirty percent (30%) as a platform fee for hosting, payment processing, customer support, and platform maintenance.

- Earnings are calculated and reported weekly
- Payments are processed every Friday for the previous week's earnings
- Minimum payout threshold is fifty dollars ($50.00)
- Earnings below the threshold accumulate and carry forward to the following week

### 3.3 Payment Methods

The Contractor may select from the following payment methods:
- Direct bank transfer (ACH)
- PayPal
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum)

### 3.4 Taxes and Withholding

The Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes on compensation received. The Company will not withhold taxes from payments. The Contractor will receive a Form 1099-NEC if annual earnings exceed $600.
